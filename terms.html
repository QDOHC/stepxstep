Step x Step is a free app for running and step challenges between friends. By creating an account and using the app, you agree to these terms.

Your account

You need an email address and a password to use the app. Keep your password to yourself and use one you do not use anywhere else.

You are responsible for what happens on your account. Your display name is seen by the people in your groups, so keep it friendly.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Account > Delete account.

Groups and challenges

Anyone with a group invite code can join that group. Only share codes with people you know. Codes stop working after 7 days.

Group admins can create challenges, make other members admins and remove members.

Be fair. Only sync your own runs and steps, and use Run or walk? to correct a walk that was saved as a run.

Don't use the app to harass, threaten or embarrass anyone, or to post anything offensive in names, group names, run plans or notes. The app owner may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

If someone breaks these rules, report them: tap them on a leaderboard (or More in the Members list) and choose Report. You can also block them. The app owner reviews every report.

Health and safety

Step x Step is for fun and motivation. It is not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise routine if you have any health concerns.

You take part in runs, walks and planned group runs at your own risk. Look after yourself, stay aware of traffic and your surroundings, and only meet people you trust.

Step x Step

Running and step challenges with your friends.

Make a group, invite your friends with a code, and take on monthly or custom challenges: running distance, distance on foot (runs and walks), or steps. Tap Sync to bring in your workouts and steps from Apple Health or Health Connect, and see the leaderboard by day, week or month.

Others in your group only ever see your totals. No adverts, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

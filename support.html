Getting started

Sign up with your email address, then type the code from the email into the app. If it has not arrived after a minute, check your spam or junk folder, and use the code in the newest email. To join your friends' group, ask a group admin for an invite code and enter it in Groups > Join with a code. Invite codes work for 7 days.

Sync is not bringing in my runs, walks or steps

  • iPhone: open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps > Step x Step, and turn on Workouts, Walking + Running Distance and Steps.
  • Android: open Health Connect, tap App permissions, choose Step x Step, and allow Exercise, Distance and Steps.

Then tap Sync again. Only workouts recorded as runs, walks or hikes count, and nothing is ever counted twice.

Reporting someone

Tap the person on a leaderboard (or More next to them in a group's Members list) and choose Report or Block. Every report is reviewed. You can also email us.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. This removes your sign-in, profile, group memberships, runs, walks, steps and sync status straight away. It cannot be undone. Groups and challenges you created stay for the other members, without your name.

If you no longer have the app, email step.x.step2026@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. We delete it, with all the data above, within 7 days and reply to confirm.

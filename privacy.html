Privacy policy

LAST UPDATED 27 OCTOBER 2026

Step x Step reads as little as it can, keeps your individual runs and steps private, and never sells or shares your data.

What the app reads from your phone

Only your running, walking and hiking workouts (start time, end time and distance) and your step counts as hourly totals, from Apple Health or Health Connect.

It reads only when you tap Sync, never in the background, and never writes anything to your health app.

It never reads your GPS routes, heart rate, sleep, location or any other health data.

What the app keeps

Your email address and password (used only to sign in), your display name, your privacy settings, the groups and challenges you join, your planned group runs and your answers to them, your imported runs, walks and hourly step totals, the result of your last sync, high-fives sent and received, the people you block, and any reports you send.

Reports are only read by the app owner, to keep the app safe. The person reported is not told who reported them. Only you can see who you blocked.

Your phone remembers which pop-ups you have already seen (the welcome guide, badges, recaps). That stays on the phone.

Your sign-in on the phone is kept in its secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).

Who can see what

Only you can see your individual runs and walks and your hourly steps.

People in a challenge with you see your display name and your totals for each day, week and month of it: distance and number of runs (or runs and walks) in distance challenges, and steps in steps challenges. The ticks for each day of your week are only shown to you.

People in your groups see the runs planned in that group and how many people are going. Your name only shows on your answers if you turn on Show my name on planned runs in Settings > Privacy (it is off to start with).

Badges and your weekly recap are worked out from your own runs, walks and steps and are only shown to you, unless you choose to share the recap picture yourself.

When you high-five someone, they see your name and the group. Nothing else is sent.

Services the app uses

Supabase stores the app data and handles sign-in.

Sign-up and password emails are sent through Gmail.

When you type a meeting place for a planned run, the text you type is sent to Photon (photon.komoot.io, built on OpenStreetMap) to suggest addresses, along with a rough map position already known for the group. Nothing else about you is sent.

There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Your choices

Delete any run, walk or hike in Settings > Runs and walks, or mark a run as a walk so it stops counting as running.

Delete all your steps, or disconnect health access, in Settings > Health and sync.

Leave a group at any time. Your runs and steps stay yours and stop showing on that group’s leaderboards.

Block someone from a leaderboard or the Members list (More > Block). Unblock them in Settings > Privacy.

Deleting your account

Settings > Account > Delete account removes your sign-in, profile, group memberships, runs, steps and sync status straight away. It cannot be undone.

Groups and challenges you created stay for the other members, without your name.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
